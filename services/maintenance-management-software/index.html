Questions we get a lot

Is this a CMMS?

It does the job most small and medium teams want from a CMMS: complaints in, jobs assigned, work tracked and reported. It just skips the 200-page manual. We shape it around how your maintenance team already works.

Do my technicians need training?

No. If your staff need training, we built it wrong. Technicians see three buttons: Start, Photo, Done.

Can it handle several buildings or clients?

Yes. Jobs are organised by area and department, supervisors see only their own block, and you see everything. Facility companies can track each client separately with deadlines and monthly reports.

How much does it cost?

Fixed price. After a free 30-minute Problem Hunt and a one-week audit, we hand you three tool ideas, each with a fixed price, before you commit to anything.
