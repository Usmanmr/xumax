Questions we get a lot

Do patients need an app?

No. Everything happens on WhatsApp, which patients already use.

Can each doctor see their own schedule?

Yes. Each doctor, stylist or room has its own calendar, and reception sees them all.

What about patient privacy?

We keep patient data to what the booking needs and agree how it’s stored during the audit, in line with the privacy rules where you operate.
