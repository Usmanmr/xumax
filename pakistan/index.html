WhatsApp first, because that’s where your business already runs

Most Pakistani businesses run on WhatsApp groups: complaints, orders, approvals, all mixed together. We don’t fight that. Our tools take requests from WhatsApp and turn them into jobs with an owner, a status and a deadline.

Urdu, English and everything in between

Your staff and customers write in Urdu, English and Roman Urdu, often in the same message. Our AI bits understand “2 karahi, 1 extra spicy” and “AC kaam nahi kar raha” alike.

Local payments and the way you get paid

Where an app needs payments, we design around what your customers already use, such as JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast transfers, bank transfer and cash on delivery.

Close enough to visit, priced for local budgets

Fixed prices agreed upfront, no per-user fees, and a team in your time zone. Load-shedding and patchy signal? Our apps are built to save work and send it when the connection comes back.
