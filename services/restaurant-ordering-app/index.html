Questions we get a lot

Will this replace delivery aggregators?

It doesn’t have to. Many restaurants keep aggregators for new customers and move regulars to their own WhatsApp ordering, where there’s no commission per order.

Do customers need to download an app?

No. Customers order on WhatsApp, which they already use. If you also want a branded app or website ordering, we can scope that in the audit.

Can the kitchen use it during a rush?

Yes. The kitchen screen shows three buttons per order: Accepted, Ready, Sent. Nothing to learn in the middle of a Friday night rush.

How long does it take?

A clickable prototype in 1 to 2 weeks after the audit, and live in weeks, not months, at a fixed price.
